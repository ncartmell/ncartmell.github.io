Outside

The rest of it. Everything on this site is otherwise about work, and that is not the whole picture.

Running

Running since 2022 — mostly road, with some trail. Several half marathons so far and a marathon in training, plus the local parkrun when I want the shorter version of the same thing.

It is for fitness and general wellbeing more than anything else, but having a race in the diary is what actually keeps it going. I am competitive enough to mind how it goes, which turns out to be the useful part. Strava has the log.

Magic: The Gathering

Mostly Commander, which is the four-player format where games run long and everyone is keeping track of rather a lot at once. Some draft alongside it, prereleases when a set is worth turning up for, and a bit of Standard online. I build more decks than I get to sleeve up; they are on Archidekt.

The life tracker on the projects page came out of playing Commander — a scoreboard for four people turns out to be a more interesting problem than it sounds.

Games

Mostly on Steam, and a long stretch of Destiny 2 in particular — enough of one that the Discord bot on the projects page ended up with a Bungie API client behind it, largely so I could stop answering the same questions by hand.
